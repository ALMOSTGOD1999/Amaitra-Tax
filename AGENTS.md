## Application rules
- Maintain one route file per public content section (currently nine, including the Our Team page and the Future Dreams and Tradx Equisearch sister ventures) with shared site chrome; each leaf owns its metadata so every section is independently shareable.
- Keep reusable business details, service content and metadata helpers in a browser-safe site module to prevent page-to-page inconsistencies.
- Render the welcome particles with a client-only canvas and clean up animation frames; this supports hundreds of objects without hundreds of animated DOM nodes.
- Contact inquiries prepare an SMS for the supplied phone, with explicit user confirmation; do not imply that a message was sent or invent an email endpoint.
- Keep visual styling in semantic global tokens and site classes, with corporate Button variants for consistent controls.
